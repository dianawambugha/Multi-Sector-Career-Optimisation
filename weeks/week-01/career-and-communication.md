## 150–200 word decision brief

Use results from your own queries. Label them as a synthetic teaching example.

1. **Problem:** What decision does the credit manager need to make?
2. **Evidence:** State two checked findings with reporting date, units, and denominator.
3. **Implication:** Explain what the findings suggest, and distinguish that from a proven cause.
4. **Action:** Suggest a proportionate next investigation or action.
5. **Limitation:** Explain missing snapshots, the tiny sample, and why delinquency is not itself a probability of default or an IFRS 9 ECL estimate.
